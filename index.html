Getting connected
  1. Plug the adapter into the OBD2 port under the dash, left of the steering column.
  2. Start the engine (or turn the key to ON).
  3. Android: open this app in Chrome. iPhone: open it in the free Bluefy browser.
  4. Tap Connect and pick the adapter. The Vgate iCar Pro usually shows up as IOS-Vlink or Android-Vlink. Don't pair it in phone settings first.
  5. Skip the list every time (Android): in Chrome open chrome://flags, search Web Bluetooth new permissions backend, set it to Enabled and restart Chrome. After one more Connect, the app reconnects to your car by itself when it opens.
Backup

Everything is saved only on this phone. Save a backup file to move your cars, records, oil log and mileage to another phone, or between Chrome and the MyDash app.
